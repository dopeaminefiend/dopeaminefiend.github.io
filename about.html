About REDTEAM

Made in
Toronto,
for Toronto.

REDTEAM is an independent quarterly print magazine published out of Toronto, Ontario. We cover design, culture, housing, music, food, and the politics of living in a city that keeps changing faster than most people can keep up with.

We started in 2022 because we wanted a magazine that felt like it was actually about Toronto — not a generic city-life brand with a Toronto flag slapped on it. Every issue is reported, photographed, and designed in the city.

REDTEAM is reader-funded. We don't take programmatic advertising and we're not backed by a media conglomerate. If you buy a copy, you're keeping this thing alive. That's the whole deal.

We publish four times a year. Submissions for Issue 05 are open now. See the submissions page for guidelines.
